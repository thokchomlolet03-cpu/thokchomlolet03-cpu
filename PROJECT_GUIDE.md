## Background and approach

I spent approximately three years in Level 2 enterprise IT support for Finastra through Tech Mahindra (2020–2023), working with end-user devices, Microsoft 365, identity/access, endpoint tooling, incident management, and cross-team escalations. Alongside work, I have continued building software, infrastructure labs, learning resources, and exploratory research projects.

I use projects to investigate real problems, make assumptions explicit, build a working implementation, test it, and document what remains uncertain.

## How I document work

For substantial projects, I aim to include:

1. The problem, users, assumptions, and boundaries.
2. What runs today versus what is proposed or simulated.
3. Architecture, dependencies, reproducible setup, and operational costs.
4. Tests, failures, limitations, and unresolved questions.
5. Traceable code, decisions, and evidence for changes.
