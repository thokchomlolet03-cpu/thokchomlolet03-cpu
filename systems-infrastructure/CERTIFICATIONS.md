## Decisions, not accumulation
- Start with free resources. **Do not book any paid exam in the first 90 days by default**.
- First paid credential: **choose one**: RHCSA if Linux/sysadmin interviews materialize, **or** MD-102 if endpoint/Intune positions respond. Existing enterprise endpoint experience provides a stronger early narrative for MD-102, but technical interest may favor RHCSA.
- Jamf 100 offers a relatively inexpensive Apple-specific knowledge signal; don't claim full enterprise Jamf competence from online simulations.
- CCNA is valuable for network-intensive roles, but you might gain greater hiring benefit from endpoint or Linux credentials sooner.
- Large paid UAE workshops: require hands-on equipment, exam-relevant objectives, qualified instructor and transparent total cost.
- Certificate renewal/expiry terms differ; maintain correct dates and public verification IDs if comfortable sharing.
