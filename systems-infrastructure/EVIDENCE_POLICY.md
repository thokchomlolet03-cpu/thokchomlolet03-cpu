# Evidence, integrity, safety and public presentation

This repository serves learners and hiring managers. The central promise is **no misleading project claims**.

## Strict distinctions
- **Curriculum authored:** a plan, not evidence of course attendance or understanding.
- **Source code authored:** not necessarily executed, safe, effective or production-ready.
- **Lab demonstration:** executed on described authorized environment with real outputs and repeatable procedure.
- **Certification earned:** exam passed, credential issued and correctly named.
- **Production experience:** genuinely performed professionally; disclosed only within legitimate confidentiality limits.
- **Independently verified:** another person or controlled reproduction supports the claim.

## Before publishing
- Remove names, emails, passwords, API tokens, internal hostnames/IPs, private certs, employee/customer IDs, legal names of customers and production ticket details.
- Review packet captures, screenshots, stack traces, environment files and process command lines for accidental secrets.
- Do not post proprietary employer training, ARRiSE Learning Hub materials, customer configs, protected course solutions, or third-party copyrighted text.
- Do not paste full Linux `env`, shell history or cloud metadata service responses into public issues.
- Show measured performance only with units, platform, version, trial count, conditions and raw method.
- Label lab names/data as synthetic.
- Avoid exposure of insecure practice environments: use private local networks and snapshots; never scan unauthorized endpoints.

## Professional narrative
A hiring manager can trust an incomplete portfolio with rigorous evidence more than a flashy collection of invented accomplishments. Show failures, wrong hypotheses, revised explanations and legitimate limitations.

## Tool and AI usage
AI may draft materials and source, but authorship alone is not evidence. AI-generated content should be reviewed, corrected and demonstrated by the learner. Human review and independent testing are needed before claiming competency.

### Case-study publication checklist
- [ ] Real run occurred
- [ ] Reproducible steps and versions documented
- [ ] Test artifacts visible (redacted)
- [ ] Failure / recovery verified
- [ ] No sensitive or licensed content
- [ ] Limitation and status updated
- [ ] Link added to recruiter evidence index
