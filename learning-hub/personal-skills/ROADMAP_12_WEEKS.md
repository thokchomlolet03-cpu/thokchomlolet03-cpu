# Twelve-week human-skills practice plan

[← Human skills hub](README.md) · [Resources](RESOURCE_CATALOG.csv) · [Practice log](PRACTICE_LOG.csv)

**A flexible 12-session sequence, not a deadline.** Suggested budget: **two to four hours per week**, depending on work and other commitments. The goal is one real-world practice and a written observation each week—not accumulating courses or risking savings.

## How to use the plan

- First session each week: read/watch from *one* selected source.
- Second session: create something observable—one before/after, a small conversation, a written decision or an interview question.
- Third short session: compare **prediction vs actual evidence**, revise and record only non-sensitive facts.
- If a week is demanding, complete one smaller task and **continue the sequence later**. This is not a challenge to compete with your systems engineering and German studies.

## Four milestone checks

**After week 4:** Can you explain composition, silhouette and color choices without vague labels such as "just looks better"? Can you identify one actual readability/accessibility improvement?

**After week 6:** Have you tested two small actions within a firm budget and protected essential responsibilities? Can you distinguish a real unacceptable risk from mere uncertainty?

**After week 9:** Can a listener explain what your technical project actually demonstrated? Are your results and limits truthful? Have you made one concise relevant contribution to a discussion?

**After week 12:** Do you have a user problem statement informed by real observations, an ethically conducted feedback loop, and at least one revised visual or communication artifact?

## Completion integrity

Course enrollment is not completion. Watching a lecture is not competence. Hypothetical customer interviews are not market validation. This is a **learning curriculum only**—the log starts empty until independent practice is performed.
