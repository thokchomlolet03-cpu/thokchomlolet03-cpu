# Human skills learning track — design, judgment, communication, and entrepreneurship

[← Learning Hub](../README.md) · [Start Here](../../START_HERE.md) · [NotebookLM](../notebooklm/INDEX.md) · [12-week roadmap](ROADMAP_12_WEEKS.md)

**Purpose:** a public, practical learning library for four complementary abilities: interpreting visual design, making thoughtful decisions under uncertainty, explaining work to others, and understanding customers. This track is **educational and exploratory**, separate from verified employment experience and engineering-lab evidence.

**Published:** November 2026. None of the courses or exercises below is marked completed. Source prices, schedules and availability can change.

## The four abilities connect

~~~text
Observe what is visually coherent ─────┐
                                       ├──> Design something understandable
Communicate needs and evidence ────────┤
                                       ├──> Earn feedback and professional trust
Experiment under bounded risk ─────────┤
                                       └──> Understand a real customer problem
Marketing and customer discovery ──────┘
~~~

This is not a claim that aesthetics has objective universal rules, that frequent speaking guarantees promotions, or that studying marketing guarantees a viable business. It is a systematic way to improve judgments and decisions using **practice and feedback**.

## Use this as a one-click daily launcher

| Available time | What to open | Practice |
|---|---|---|
| **15 minutes** | [NN/g visual design principles](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/principles-visual-design/) | Compare two layouts and name the focal point and contrast |
| **20 minutes** | [CCI Assertiveness](https://www.cci.health.wa.gov.au/Resources/Looking-After-Yourself/Assertiveness) | Rewrite a vague contribution as problem → action → result |
| **20 minutes** | [CCI Procrastination](https://www.cci.health.wa.gov.au/Resources/Looking-After-Yourself/Procrastination) | Plan a low-cost experiment, explicitly list its limits |
| **30 minutes** | [HubSpot Inbound](https://academy.hubspot.com/courses/inbound) | Distinguish a customer problem from a proposed product feature |
| **60 minutes** | [Y Combinator Startup School](https://www.startupschool.org/) | Prepare non-leading questions for a genuine customer conversation |

Choose *one* activity; don't try to do everything daily. For a suggested sequence, use [12 weeks](ROADMAP_12_WEEKS.md).

## Your study system

1. Follow a source in [RESOURCE_CATALOG.csv](RESOURCE_CATALOG.csv).
2. Write a prediction and record a practical exercise in [PRACTICE_TEMPLATES.md](PRACTICE_TEMPLATES.md).
3. Record a short factual status in [PRACTICE_LOG.csv](PRACTICE_LOG.csv), **only after actually doing it**.
4. For deeper source analysis, add an approved **public** notebook to [NotebookLM index](../notebooklm/INDEX.md) after privacy review.
5. Evaluate feedback and choose the next tiny experiment; course completion alone is not mastery.

## Financial and privacy boundaries

- Start with free resources. Coursera access, CalArts/Wharton programs and public-speaking membership can involve charges; confirm current terms *before* committing.
- Protected experimentation means **do not risk essential income, immigration obligations, housing needs or employment** merely to prove confidence.
- This GitHub repository is public. Do not publish private worries, salary details, workplace incidents, customer identities, personal photographs, company data, private conversations, or sensitive feedback without appropriate permission.
- The Centre for Clinical Interventions self-help workbooks are educational tools, **not a diagnosis or a substitute for clinical support**. If functioning is significantly affected, qualified professional help may be appropriate.
- Personal style is about fit, coherence and preference—not trying to satisfy assumptions about height, skin tone, personality or worth.
- Avoid interviewing customers at work or accessing company data without authorization. Record anonymous, consent-based research.

### What success looks like

Better reasoning about why a design works; confident, evidence-based descriptions of actual work; one respectful contribution when relevant; clearer risk boundaries; and a real problem statement informed by customers. **No invented qualifications, metrics, conversations or successful ventures.**
