**Do not** publish interview transcripts, contact details or identifying anecdotes without permission. Five interviews can reveal useful themes; they do not establish statistical demand.

## Claim status labels

- **Planned** — resource, exercise or intended interview only.
- **Observed** — personally attempted, with a brief factual note.
- **Revised after feedback** — changed something and compared outcomes.
- **Externally assessed** — independent critique from a clearly described source.
- **Certified** — only after meeting the relevant official provider's requirements.

No "completed" claims should be introduced by an AI assistant without the learner confirming the actual work.
