**Updated 16 November 2026.** This is a quick reference, not an additional six-course completion requirement. Build real demand evidence before accumulating certificates or architecture.

## Select one immediate bottleneck

| If the problem is... | Start with this free original source | Do this in the real world |
|---|---|---|
| Not sure who needs the product | [Y Combinator Startup School](https://www.startupschool.org/) and [YC Startup Library](https://www.ycombinator.com/library) | Interview prospects about what they already do, pay and struggle with |
| Too much prototyping, no usage | [YC — How to plan an MVP](https://www.ycombinator.com/library/6f-how-to-plan-an-mvp) | Make one small useful output a person can actually try |
| Don't know how to ask for a sale | [Stripe Atlas — Your first 10 customers](https://stripe.com/guides/atlas/starting-sales) + [HubSpot Inbound Sales](https://academy.hubspot.com/courses/inbound-sales) | Ask a qualified prospect for a specific trial or paid pilot |
| Unsure what to charge | [Stripe Atlas — Pricing low-touch SaaS](https://stripe.com/guides/atlas/saas-pricing) | Test a real, clearly stated price and calculate serving costs |
| Unsure about owning a future company | [Stripe Atlas — Equity for founders](https://stripe.com/guides/atlas/equity) | Work through a hypothetical dilution table; obtain licensed professional advice before legal action |
| Need broader acquisition/marketing literacy | [HubSpot Inbound](https://academy.hubspot.com/courses/inbound) | Try one truthful communication channel, report observed results |

**Cost boundaries:** The resources above are free-to-read or free-to-study (HubSpot advertises a free Inbound Sales certification); **Stripe Atlas company formation is paid**, and Stripe's legal examples focus on US structures. UAE and India legal/tax/business-licence requirements must be checked separately before monetization.

## The practical aim

For a scientific explanation product such as Mangal Visual, don't assume every student, teacher, research institution and company is one customer category. Select one buyer segment; learn about their current alternatives and budget; offer one trustworthy result; then see whether they return or pay.

Use the [90-day execution plan and private evidence card](../entrepreneurship/README.md) instead of creating an additional course calendar. Preserve customer confidentiality, scientific integrity, source rights and employment/visa constraints.
