# German learning path — A1 → A2 → B1 → B2 → C1 → C2

[← Learning Hub](../README.md) · [NotebookLM German index](../notebooklm/INDEX.md) · [Official Goethe exam practice](https://www.goethe.de/de/spr/prf/ueb.html)

**[Learning philosophy](../LEARNING_PHILOSOPHY.md):** language competence is an ongoing foundation for wider communication and cross-cultural participation, not merely an exam to pass.

**Goal:** build German proficiency useful for daily life, IT infrastructure work, interviews and, eventually, German-language study. There is no fixed guaranteed time per level. Study and progress according to tested speaking, listening, reading and writing, not videos watched.

**Start with the method, not just the links:** [**How to learn German from Meitei through English — a detailed essay**](HOW_TO_LEARN_GERMAN.md). As a first-language Meitei speaker who is most comfortable using English for explanations, use English strategically to understand grammar, while training German listening, retrieval and communication directly. The essay covers pronunciation, word order, case, memory, deliberate practice, transfer, feedback, and realistic assessment through C2.

## A1 — Beginner

**Start:** [DW Learn German / Nicos Weg](https://learngerman.dw.com/) (A1) → [VHS A1 (direct course)](https://a1.vhs-lernportal.de/wws/home.php) → [Goethe introductory exercises](https://www.goethe.de/de/spr/ueb.html).

**Mechanisms to master:** alphabet and pronunciation, basic word order, present tense, verbs *sein/haben*, gender and articles, nominative/accusative, questions, numbers, appointments, directions and basic daily interactions.

**Output gate:** Introduce yourself, describe your day and current work, ask for directions, write a short message, and understand short slow dialogues without reading translations.

## A2 — Elementary

**Start:** [DW Nicos Weg](https://learngerman.dw.com/) (A2) → [VHS A2 direct course](https://a2.vhs-lernportal.de/wws/home.php) + [deutsch.info A2](https://deutsch.info/) → [Easy German](https://www.easygerman.org/) for accessible real conversations.

**Focus:** past tense and Perfekt, dative, separable verbs, common subordinate clauses, prepositions, health/travel/work routines. Practice describing a simple technology problem in German.

**Output gate:** Write 120–150 words about a past event and explain a basic workplace problem in connected sentences, then revise errors.

## B1 — Intermediate

**Start:** [DW Nicos Weg](https://learngerman.dw.com/) (B1) + [VHS B1 direct course](https://b1.vhs-lernportal.de/wws/home.php) + [Goethe free exercises](https://www.goethe.de/de/spr/ueb.html).

**Focus:** longer conversations, opinions and justifications, relative clauses, Konjunktiv II and everyday workplace correspondence.

**Output gate:** Give a five-minute personal explanation of an IT incident, write a concise incident summary and answer follow-up questions. Use [Goethe B1 mock exam](https://www.goethe.de/de/spr/prf/ueb.html) to expose weaknesses.

## B2 — Upper intermediate

**Start:** [VHS B2 German for work — direct course](https://b2-beruf.vhs-lernportal.de/wws/home.php), [deutsch.info B2](https://deutsch.info/), [Goethe exercises](https://www.goethe.de/de/spr/ueb.html), [Easy German podcast](https://www.easygerman.org/podcast/).

**Focus:** complex technical discussion, workplace disagreement, formal writing, detailed explanation of causes and consequences, reading German job descriptions and industry reports.

**Output gate:** Explain a networking failure with competing hypotheses in German; write an original one-page technical explanation and deliver an organized spoken answer. Compare against [Goethe B2 exam requirements](https://www.goethe.de/de/spr/prf/ueb.html).

## C1 — Advanced

**Start:** [VHS C1 German for work — direct course](https://c1-beruf.vhs-lernportal.de/wws/home.php), [Goethe advanced practice](https://www.goethe.de/de/spr/ueb.html), [Easy German](https://www.easygerman.org/podcast/) and authentic German technology articles.

**Focus:** nuanced arguments, unfamiliar accents and abstract subjects, advanced grammar, technical presentations, policy and incident-review writing.

**Output gate:** Summarize a complex German or translated technical report accurately, defend trade-offs, and deliver a 10–15-minute explanation followed by questions. Use the [Goethe C1 sample exam](https://www.goethe.de/de/spr/prf/ueb.html).

## C2 — Proficient

**Start:** [Goethe C2 practice tests](https://www.goethe.de/de/spr/prf/ueb.html), [Goethe broad advanced exercises](https://www.goethe.de/de/spr/ueb.html), authentic books, radio, interviews and German technical material. At C2, there is **no single free guided sequence recommended here**: use demanding input plus frequent native/professional feedback.

**Focus:** nuance, idiomatic language, sophisticated writing and precision under time constraints. Avoid claiming C2 solely from AI assessments.

**Output gate:** Structured argument about a complex systems topic, critical review of a long source, fluent follow-up discussion and formal practice exam performance.

## German for Systems Engineering

Use [Goethe — free German for work and everyday life](https://www.goethe.de/de/spr/ueb.html), [VHS](https://www.vhs-lernportal.de/wws/home.php) and job ads from Germany as your practical vocabulary source.

| English concept | German you should recognize |
|---|---|
| Operating system / administrator | Betriebssystem / Systemadministrator(in) |
| Network / failure | Netzwerk / Störung |
| Troubleshooting | Fehlersuche / Fehlerbehebung |
| Server / availability | Server / Verfügbarkeit |
| Backup / restoration | Datensicherung / Wiederherstellung |
| Permissions / users | Berechtigungen / Benutzer |
| Maintenance / monitoring | Wartung / Überwachung |
| Root cause / incident | Ursache / Vorfall / Störung |

Write your own short incident explanations in German. Avoid publishing employer-internal incidents in any language.

## A sustainable shift-work routine (45–60 minutes)

1. **20 min:** one lesson in your primary course; do exercises from memory.
2. **10 min:** spaced vocabulary recall (include 3–5 IT-specific terms once at A2+).
3. **10 min:** one short speaking/recording or listening shadowing session.
4. **10–20 min:** original paragraph and corrections, or a guided [NotebookLM German deep dive](../notebooklm/README.md).

**Once a week:** one exam-style speaking/writing exercise + one free Goethe sample task. **Every four weeks:** compare your work with CEFR descriptors or obtain instructor feedback. A public tracker should never assert a level until earned or independently assessed.

## Additional resources that fill real gaps

The core path already covers A1–C2, free structured learning, authentic listening and official exam samples. The following resources are **targeted additions**, not extra mandatory curricula. Choose them to solve an observed problem rather than accumulating subscriptions. Links and claims checked against official provider pages on 7 November 2026.

| Need not fully served by the current launcher | Verified resource | Access and use |
|---|---|---|
| English-friendly, academically structured German foundation with audio and phonetics | **[University of Texas at Austin — Deutsch im Blick](https://coerll.utexas.edu/dib/)** | Free open-access first-year multimedia German textbook; especially useful when an English-language explanation makes a German pattern click. Use selectively alongside DW/VHS, not as a compulsory third full course. |
| Detailed English-supported grammar explanations and self-correcting examples | **[UT Austin — Grimm Grammar](https://coerll.utexas.edu/gg/)** | Free grammar reference: noun gender, cases, conjugation, clauses, separable verbs and usage. Look up the rule after seeing it in actual German, then produce a fresh example yourself. |
| Listening discrimination and German articulation (not just repeating TTS) | **[Goethe-Institut — Online Pronunciation Training](https://www.goethe.de/en/spr/ueb/ast.html)** | Free online pronunciation training with sound-discrimination tasks, modeled production and automated feedback; registration required. Human feedback remains valuable. |
| Durable vocabulary and phrase recall across shifts | **[Anki — official desktop and mobile options](https://apps.ankiweb.net/)** · [manual](https://docs.ankiweb.net/) | Desktop and AnkiWeb are free; AnkiDroid on Android is free; official AnkiMobile on iOS is paid. Make cards for useful phrases, noun + article + plural, audio and situation-based prompts. Do not overload new cards. |
| Real-world spoken/written German for IT and technical work | **[Goethe-Institut — Deutsch am Arbeitsplatz](https://www.goethe.de/de/spr/ueb/daa.html)** | Free workplace German exercises, including science and technology; start appropriate topics around A2/B1 and continue into higher levels as comprehension permits. Useful for colleagues, calls, reports and troubleshooting explanations. |
| A consistent definition of progress independent of any learning app | **[Council of Europe — CEFR descriptors](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/cefr-descriptors)** | Free can-do descriptions for reception, production, interaction and mediation. Use as a rubric with external feedback, not as a self-issued language certificate. |

**What not to add for now:** another general A1–B1 video course, multiple duplicate flashcard tools, or a paid AI-conversation subscription before you have used the free backbone and identified a concrete problem. A qualified human tutor, conversation partner or suitable in-person intensive course can become the next *high-value addition* for listening and spontaneous response. Verify examination recognition, registration and fees with the actual employer or university separately.

**Mechanism checklist for each new lesson:** (1) predict meaning before opening English subtitles, (2) notice one new pattern and explain it briefly in English, (3) say or write an original German answer *before* checking the model, (4) correct the biggest misunderstanding, and (5) test the same idea in a different context on another day. Record independent attempts separately from supported retries. [Why this works and where it fails →](HOW_TO_LEARN_GERMAN.md).
