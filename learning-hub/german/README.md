# German — one strong main course, real practice, clear milestones

[← Learning Hub](../README.md) · [How to learn German, deeply](HOW_TO_LEARN_GERMAN.md) · [Official Goethe exam practice](https://www.goethe.de/de/spr/prf/ueb.html)

**Reorganized 16 November 2026.** The priority is German ability you can use, not collecting competing A1 courses. This is a self-study plan; no independently assessed CEFR level is claimed.

## One primary course

**[Deutsche Welle — Nicos Weg](https://learngerman.dw.com/)** is the primary A1 → A2 → B1 program. Do each lesson's comprehension and recall exercises; write or say something new without subtitles and return to errors after a day. Do **not** complete a second A1 textbook in parallel merely because it is free.

| If you need... | Best free official supplement | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| More grammar/vocabulary practice | **[Goethe-Institut free exercises](https://www.goethe.de/de/spr/ueb.html)** | After a specific error in DW; choose a focused exercise |
| A different full teaching sequence | **[VHS Lernportal](https://www.vhs-lernportal.de/wws/home.php)** | Only if DW stops working after experimenting with lesson method; not a second compulsory curriculum |
| Clear English explanation of rules | **[UT Austin — Grimm Grammar](https://coerll.utexas.edu/gg/)** or **[Deutsch im Blick](https://coerll.utexas.edu/dib/)** | Look up one confusing German pattern; produce your own examples |
| Pronunciation feedback | **[Goethe Pronunciation Training](https://www.goethe.de/en/spr/ueb/ast.html)** | When you cannot reliably hear or produce a contrast |
| Real German life and conversation | **[Easy German](https://www.easygerman.org/)** (free videos; some exercises paid) | Listening/culture alongside a primary DW lesson |
| Objective level check | **[Official Goethe sample exams](https://www.goethe.de/de/spr/prf/ueb.html)** and **[CEFR descriptors](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/cefr-descriptors)** | After practice, to find specific gaps; neither is self-issued certification |

## Progress by capability, not promised calendar time

| Stage | Main work | Independent outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **A1** | DW A1; sounds, word order, present tense, basic cases and questions | Introduce yourself, describe a normal day and ask for help with a simple situation |
| **A2** | DW A2; everyday past tense, separable verbs, dative and subordinate clauses | Explain something that happened at work and handle common transactions |
| **B1** | DW B1; extended listening, linking explanations and longer sentences | Narrate an IT problem and write a corrected incident description |
| **B2** | When B1 output is genuinely reliable, use **[VHS German for work](https://www.vhs-lernportal.de/wws/home.php)**, authentic reports, German discussions and Goethe practice | Defend a technical explanation, discuss trade-offs and write clear professional correspondence |
| **C1–C2** | Authentic complex material, high-quality instructor/human feedback and official exam practice | Sustain nuanced discussion and independently meet exam can-do requirements. No single free full C2 curriculum is promised here |

## Routine for rotating shifts

- **Active study (about 45 minutes):** 20 minutes DW lesson + exercises, 10 minutes recall from memory, 10 minutes original spoken/written response, 5 minutes correct your largest error.
- **Passive bus commute:** native German vlogs, ordinary conversations and cultural programs; enjoy them as exposure, **not** a substitute for active retrieval.
- **Once a week:** record a short explanation or conversation and obtain external correction where possible.
- **Every few weeks:** compare new independent outputs with older ones; only claim a tested level after appropriate assessment.

The extended essay [How to Learn German](HOW_TO_LEARN_GERMAN.md) explains why perception, retrieval, pronunciation, feedback and actual interaction matter. Refer there for methods; this page is the concise launchpad.

Free DW, VHS or Goethe practice is not the same as an official Goethe exam or a CEFR certificate. Do not publish private conversations without consent.
