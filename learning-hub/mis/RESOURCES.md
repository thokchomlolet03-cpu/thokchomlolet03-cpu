## Textbook and library strategy
OpenStax and OpenIntro are foundational reading, USF for methods. Fundamentals of BPM supplementary materials are freely linked; buy/borrow the associated book only if required. CMU lists Database System Concepts (Silberschatz, Korth, Sudarshan) as a reference; library access may be needed. Use older MIT Sloan case frameworks alongside modern NIST and cloud material, not obsolete vendor details.

## Extension only after core mastery
- Process mining: [TU Eindhoven course description](https://research.tue.nl/en/courses/fundamentals-of-process-mining/); find current public teaching and exercises separately, not assumed accessible.
- Research orientation: Münster information systems thinking and business process management, Queen's IS theories and methods, Ottawa IT management elective/research themes (see benchmark links).
- Security/network deepening: Illinois State MSIS sequence descriptions. Reuse systems workbench; no certification exam required for this syllabus.
- Formal public course completion certificates should be recorded separately from academic credits and skill evidence.
