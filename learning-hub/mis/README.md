2. Take the closed-book [baseline diagnostic](ASSESSMENT.md#baseline-diagnostic).

## Workload

82 active weeks × 15 h = 1,230 hours, plus 8 flexible weeks (revision, shift work, applications, missed labs). Starting late November 2026 points to summer 2028. Time is a planning budget, not an academic equivalency claim. Only skip where diagnostics and delayed recall support it.

Weekly: 4 h primary readings/lectures, 5 h lab, 3 h closed-book problem solving, 2 h written case/research, 1 h spaced review. Prefer depth over collecting credentials. Use official free resources first; provider exams, textbooks and cloud compute may cost money. Protect daily work and sleep.

## Rules of mastery

- For every concept: predict, implement, observe, explain failure and compare alternatives.
- Target 90/100 per module, all safety/security checks passed, repeat after 2–4 weeks. Evidence must be personally produced and reproducible; AI can critique, not sit exams.
- Study old MIT course principles alongside modern vendor documentation, not obsolete deployment instructions.
- Produce short case memos, reports, diagrams, original labs and an oral defense. Do not post private employer/customer data, secrets or copyrighted course materials.
- No grade, certification, academic credit or publication is claimed without independent evidence.
