# Mathematics that unlocks my career — not a complete school syllabus

[← Learning Hub](../README.md) · [Free course directory](../WORLD_CLASS_FREE_COURSES.md) · [Systems roadmap](../../systems-infrastructure/ROADMAP.md)

**Revised 16 November 2026.** Comfortable through roughly Grade 10, with possible gaps from Grades 11–12. Rebuild only the prerequisites needed for infrastructure, networking, data/AI, finance and eventual research. No mathematics certificate is required to call yourself ready for a systems job; competence is shown in calculations and reasoning.

## Start with a diagnostic, not a long repeat

**Course 1: [Khan Academy — Get ready for Algebra 2](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/get-ready-for-algebra-ii).** Attempt its course challenge. Review only missed units, then go to [Khan Algebra 2](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/algebra2) for equations, functions, graphs, exponential/logarithmic models and mathematical notation.

Use [OpenStax free mathematics textbooks](https://openstax.org/subjects/math) for careful derivations when an explanation does not click. Do not spend weeks repeating arithmetic or mastered Grade 10 problems.

**Exit gate:** independently solve a simultaneous equation, interpret a function graph, use logarithms to reverse exponential growth and explain units in a cost/capacity problem.

## Stop when mathematics has done its job

- **Systems / Windows / Linux / network / cloud role:** confidently apply rows **1–2** plus percentages, rates, charts and basic statistics. You do not need calculus or an entire MIT math program to apply for IT jobs.
- **Data science, Kaggle and ML:** add **row 3**, then **row 4**; use **row 5** when reasoning about optimization, gradients or continuous models.
- **Financial/business judgment:** work through the parallel finance row as soon as algebra and percentages are stable.
- **Quantum or more advanced scientific research:** after matrices and calculus, study complex numbers, eigenvectors, tensor products and deeper probability through the existing [quantum foundation](../quantum/FOUNDATIONS.md).
- **Algorithms / mathematical proofs:** [MIT 6.042J Mathematics for Computer Science](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-042j-mathematics-for-computer-science-spring-2015/) is an **optional extension**, not the starting point for binary arithmetic. MIT lists **single-variable calculus as a prerequisite** for the full subject.

**Not on the active path:** full advanced trigonometry, extensive integration tricks, differential equations, abstract algebra, number theory proofs, multivariable calculus and specialist numerical methods. Revisit any of them when a real engineering or scientific task demands them.

## Study protocol

**Two or three 35–50-minute sessions each week** is sufficient to begin; adjust for shift work. Per session: 10 minutes to understand the mechanism, 20–30 minutes of *unaided* exercises, then explain the result and link it to a networking, data or finance problem.

Keep a short error record: *my prediction → actual calculation → source or counterexample → corrected explanation*. Proceed based on performance on unfamiliar questions, not video completion or an invented exam credential.

**First action:** attempt the Khan Academy **Get ready for Algebra 2** course challenge; make a list of missed concepts. Next action comes from actual mistakes, not an assumption that every Grade 11–12 chapter is missing.
