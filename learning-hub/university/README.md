**Purpose:** build serious subject knowledge using the publicly available teaching of well-known universities, and make the results defensible through actual assignments, independent experiments, and accurately named credentials. This is part of a [lifelong learning system](../LEARNING_PHILOSOPHY.md) pursuing **breadth and depth without a race**. Credentials are useful when they open a real door; understanding and independent ability matter even when no certificate is awarded. **This directory is a plan, not a list of completed courses or institutional affiliations.**

Always confirm the current official page before relying on enrollment or a credential.

## Free is not the same as credentialed

| Class | What it means | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Free course + conditional free provider certificate** | Meet graded/assessed work requirements; certificate belongs to the course provider, **not an accredited degree** | Harvard's [CS50 Cybersecurity](https://cs50.harvard.edu/cybersecurity/certificate/), [CS50 AI](https://cs50.harvard.edu/ai/certificate/), [CS50 SQL](https://cs50.harvard.edu/sql/certificate/) |
| **Free study, conditional university-managed academic record** | Course may support recorded credits under distinct enrollment, deadlines and applicable terms; **never assume credits transfer or are automatically free** | [University of Helsinki — DevOps with Docker](https://opin.fi/en/study-options/HY/devops-with-docker/32fc688c-76c7-42a7-b316-540a063deee7) |
| **Free course + conditional non-degree statement/badge** | Evidence of continuing study rather than a university degree or credit | Open University's [communication](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/money-business/effective-communication-the-workplace) and [entrepreneurship](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/money-business/entrepreneurship-ideas-reality) |
| **Free course content, no university completion certificate** | Self-study using public course material; make your own testable artifact | MIT OpenCourseWare, public Stanford/CMU course sites, Open Yale lectures, UT Austin German textbook |
| **Audit potentially free; verified certificate paid** | May see selected teaching content for free under provider conditions; do not list a certificate as included | Harvard Online [Entrepreneurship in Emerging Economies](https://harvardonline.harvard.edu/course/entrepreneurship-emerging-economies) and many edX/Coursera courses |

**Enrollment is not admission.** Self-directed MIT material is not an MIT degree transcript; a CS50 Certificate is **not accredited Harvard academic coursework**; OpenLearn badges are generally not Open University academic-credit awards. For university admissions, ask each institution how it evaluates these specific credentials.

## How to prove what a course taught

For *each* course, a recruiter or admissions reader should be able to open:
1. The course's **official source URL**, provider and date accessed.
2. **Actual status**: not started, studying, work submitted, passed, or formal credit recorded.
3. **What was assessed**, which credential was awarded (if any), and a public verification URL **only if actually issued**.
4. One **original, authorized** artifact: lab, explanation, analysis, practice recording or project.
5. A reflection on a failed approach, external feedback and a defensible next step.

Use [EVIDENCE_TEMPLATE.md](EVIDENCE_TEMPLATE.md) and [EVIDENCE_TRACKER.csv](EVIDENCE_TRACKER.csv). A green CI job checks the code it runs; it does not prove the student completed a university course.
