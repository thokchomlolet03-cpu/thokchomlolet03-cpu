### The "23 ECTS" number from the source text is a *potential sum*, not an achieved qualification

The shared analysis added **5 + 5 + 3 + 5 + 3 + 2 = 23 ECTS** using Helsinki Intro Python, Helsinki Advanced Python, Docker, Kubernetes, Cyber Security Base Intro MOOC, and Turku AI & Cybersecurity.

- This is correct **arithmetic for advertised course values**, but **not proof of eligibility**, credits awarded, or a formally approved 23-ECTS study package.
- Separate enrollment, assessment, course availability, identity/record registration, and transfer decisions apply to each institution. **Do not** assume an Indian BBA becomes equivalent to a CS bachelor's degree through accumulating ECTS.
- These courses overlap in breadth and deadlines; there is **no reason to pursue all simultaneously**. Existing professional Python skills may justify testing into the advanced material rather than repeating the easiest units.
- **2026 Helsinki Python conflict:** the MOOC website says it closes at end of 2026, while the Open University advertises €0 registration through 31 Aug 2027 and the course's exam schedule includes 2027 dates. Confirm which learning/examination material will actually remain available before planning a 2027 finish.
- A [University of Helsinki Python MOOC FAQ](https://programming-26.mooc.fi/faq/) says learners can complete the free course and obtain a certificate; recorded credits require passing and then registering the completed course through Open University. It does not make every participant a University of Helsinki degree student.
- **Turku registration from outside Finland:** [its official registration instructions](https://www.utu.fi/en/open-university-studies/registration) say learners without Finnish online banking credentials may skip Suomi.fi login and enter personal information. This is encouraging, but still confirm that your own details and identification can be accepted for an official study record.

## Currently restricted: Metropolia University of Applied Sciences (Open UAS)

[**Metropolia's official current rule**](https://www.metropolia.fi/en/study-at-metropolia/open-university): as of **2 Nov 2026**, a Finnish personal identity code is **required** for general Open UAS enrollment, apart from specified admission-related and U!REKA exceptions. If you study from the UAE and do not hold the required code or qualify for an exception, **do not assume you can register just because the course shows "€0."**

The [Metropolia free/nonstop ICT catalog](https://www.metropolia.fi/en/study-at-metropolia/open-university/nonstop-virtual-studies-information-and-communication-technology) lists attractive **restricted/watchlist** choices: Unix/Linux Basics & Advanced **8 ECTS**, Introduction to Operating Systems **5 ECTS**, CCNA Training Package **16 ECTS**, C Programming, Microprocessors and Discrete Mathematics, among others. They are **not** in the immediately actionable list above; ask admissions whether an exception applies before committing.

Do not claim Metropolia admission or enrollment on the basis of this catalog.

## What to produce instead of a long certificate list

1. **Programming:** original Python log parser with tests, safe synthetic input and an explanation of data structures.
2. **Networking/security:** owned test-network threat model, identifying root cause, remediation and evidence; never attack external systems.
3. **Containers:** a service failure and recovery lab with configuration, networking and storage observations.
4. **Cloud:** explain authentication boundaries, least privilege and a realistic incident without inventing cloud production experience.
5. **Academic record:** if ECTS is actually awarded, retain the official transcript privately and link only safe public proof.
