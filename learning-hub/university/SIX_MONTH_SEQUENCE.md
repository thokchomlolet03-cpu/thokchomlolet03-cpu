# First six months — manageable academic preparation (November 2026 to April/May 2027)

[← University Learning](README.md) · [Course catalog](COURSE_CATALOG.csv) · [Evidence tracker](EVIDENCE_TRACKER.csv)

**Choose one substantial technical course at a time.** The purpose is demonstrable engineering learning alongside employment and German study. Dates are planning suggestions; university deadlines are separate and must be confirmed. Do not use this schedule as a claim that you have enrolled.

## November–December 2026: time-sensitive container foundations

**Primary:** [University of Helsinki — DevOps with Docker](https://opin.fi/en/study-options/HY/devops-with-docker/32fc688c-76c7-42a7-b316-540a063deee7/implementations/761b9a25-a3b9-45bd-b8be-a956a827134f).

Why first: the current free participation window is open. The official listing gives **15 January 2027** as the work-completion condition for academic-credit eligibility and registration through **15 February 2027**; actual credit registration requires following the official terms. Do not assume a certificate or credit will be awarded merely by accessing the MOOC.

**Deliverable:** set up a Linux VM or permitted local containers, demonstrate networking and volumes, then reproduce a service outage and its safe repair. Link independently recorded evidence to [P03](../../systems-infrastructure/projects/p03-service-failure-lab/README.md) or [P13](../../systems-infrastructure/projects/p13-virtualization-recovery/README.md) only if actually executed.

**Optional light companion:** one [Open University Communication](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/money-business/effective-communication-the-workplace) lesson a week, without trying to rush its full 24-hour program.

## January–February 2027: security and verifiable course assessment

**Primary:** [Harvard CS50 — Introduction to Cybersecurity](https://cs50.harvard.edu/cybersecurity/).

Complete all problems and final project to the provider's required standard. A **free CS50 Certificate** can be earned if each qualifying item meets the stated 70% threshold; paid verified edX credential is a different product.

**Deliverable:** a synthetic threat model and secure-configuration review of an *owned* test device or VM. Do not publish vulnerabilities, screenshots or secrets from any employer-managed systems.

**Parallel small habit:** independently explain one course topic in two minutes, following [MIT How to Speak](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-tll-005-how-to-speak-january-iap-2018/).

## March–April 2027: computer architecture foundations

**Primary:** [MIT OCW 6.004 Computation Structures](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-004-computation-structures-spring-2017/).

Focus initially on gates and finite-state machines → ISA/assembly → caching/virtual memory → interrupts and concurrency. This is **self-study**, not a free MIT certificate or academic enrollment.

**Deliverable:** one original systems architecture diagram or simulation that ties a CPU/memory mechanism to behavior observable in Linux. Record exact assumptions; avoid pretending to have run a hardware laboratory you haven't performed.

**Possible companion:** [The Open University — Design Thinking](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/science-maths-technology/design-innovation/design-thinking) for accessible visual-design exercises and a conditional free statement of participation.

## May 2027 or later: choose one fork

- **Career systems priority:** [Helsinki Kubernetes MOOC](https://www.helsinki.fi/en/admissions-and-education/open-university/open-university-studies-degree-programme/computer-science), [MIT 6.1810 Operating Systems](https://pdos.csail.mit.edu/6.1810/2026/), or [Stanford CS144 Networking](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs144/), based on current job requirements and prerequisites.
- **Scientific research priority:** [Harvard CS50 AI](https://cs50.harvard.edu/ai/) and [MIT Linear Algebra](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-06sc-linear-algebra-fall-2011/) before advanced [MIT Quantum Physics](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-04-quantum-physics-i-spring-2013/).
- **Business/communication priority:** complete [OpenLearn communication](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/money-business/effective-communication-the-workplace) or [Entrepreneurship](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/money-business/entrepreneurship-ideas-reality) and an original feedback-based exercise.

The choice should reflect your weekly capacity and next concrete opportunity. Avoid enrolling in all three forks at once.

## Weekly schedule (illustrative, not mandatory)

| Session | Activity | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| 90 minutes | University course lesson and assessed exercises | Genuine practice record or submission confirmation |
| 60–90 minutes | Independent technical lab | Source and sanitized observed output |
| 30 minutes | Review mistakes and explain in plain language | Original short report with source links |
| Optional 30 minutes | One OpenLearn communication or design lesson | Short written practice |

German and existing paid employment remain separate priorities. If tired or overcommitted, reduce weekly course hours rather than falsifying a completion target.

## Monthly gate

At month-end ask: **Did I actually complete a required assessment? Did I independently reproduce the concept? What was the real limitation?** Update [EVIDENCE_TRACKER.csv](EVIDENCE_TRACKER.csv) only with verified facts. Do not describe self-study as a degree, credit-bearing enrollment or university employment.
