# Flexible course progressions — not a six-month deadline

[← University learning](README.md) · [Learning philosophy](../LEARNING_PHILOSOPHY.md) · [Catalog](COURSE_CATALOG.csv) · [Credential distinctions](CREDENTIALS.md)

**This filename is retained so earlier links do not break. The learning objective has changed from a fixed six-month sequence to an open-ended journey.** Formal course deadlines are real, but there is no personal deadline to understand operating systems, mathematics, scientific reasoning, business, or languages.

## One path into deep systems engineering

Start from [MIT 6.004 Computation Structures](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-004-computation-structures-spring-2017/) and [MIT Missing Semester](https://missing.csail.mit.edu/2026/). Follow questions into [MIT 6.1810 Operating Systems](https://pdos.csail.mit.edu/6.1810/2026/), [Stanford CS144 Networking](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs144/), and, once ready, [MIT Distributed Systems](https://pdos.csail.mit.edu/6.5840/). Develop a real lab tied to a failure or mechanism and document results.

## One path to recognized, affordable credentials

[Harvard CS50 Cybersecurity](https://cs50.harvard.edu/cybersecurity/) and [Harvard CS50 AI](https://cs50.harvard.edu/ai/) provide **conditional free CS50 Certificates** if you actually meet the grading requirements, without conferring a Harvard degree. [Open University Communication](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/money-business/effective-communication-the-workplace), [Design Thinking](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/science-maths-technology/design-innovation/design-thinking), and [Entrepreneurship](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/money-business/entrepreneurship-ideas-reality) offer non-degree learning records under their respective completion policies.

The [Helsinki Docker MOOC](https://opin.fi/en/study-options/HY/devops-with-docker/32fc688c-76c7-42a7-b316-540a063deee7) offers free study with time-sensitive academic-credit rules for the current offering. Decide whether this actual enrollment window matters to you; if not, defer it without guilt. **Never invent recorded university credits.**

## One path to research breadth

[MIT Linear Algebra](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-06sc-linear-algebra-fall-2011/), [Harvard CS50 AI](https://cs50.harvard.edu/ai/) and [MIT Quantum Physics I](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-04-quantum-physics-i-spring-2013/) connect to [quantum computing](../quantum/README.md). Continue into a bounded experiment only when the mathematical and physical prerequisites are understood. Compare with serious classical alternatives.

## Equally important complementary skills

- **Languages:** [German A1–C2](../german/README.md) plus [University of Texas Deutsch im Blick](https://coerll.utexas.edu/dib/); study other languages when motivated by culture, relationships, work or travel. Speaking and writing feedback matter as much as certificates.
- **Presentation and professional relationships:** [Human skills](../personal-skills/PROFESSIONAL_COMMUNICATION.md), [MIT How to Speak](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-tll-005-how-to-speak-january-iap-2018/) and [conferences/communities](../participation/README.md).
- **Market and customers:** [OpenLearn Entrepreneurship](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/money-business/entrepreneurship-ideas-reality) and [customer discovery](../personal-skills/MARKETING_ENTREPRENEURSHIP.md).
- **Visual judgment:** [Design Thinking](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/science-maths-technology/design-innovation/design-thinking) and [Visual Literacy](../personal-skills/VISUAL_LITERACY.md).
- **Expert production practices:** read [Cloudflare, Netflix, GitHub and Google SRE investigations](../engineering-incidents/README.md), form competing explanations and recreate a safe small-scale failure.

## A sustainable rotating rhythm

Maintain **a deep question**, **a language practice habit**, **a lighter creative/social/business interest**, and **occasional exposure to experts**. Cycle emphasis according to actual energy and interest. A course may take two weeks or two years; this does not invalidate the aim.

When a credential could open a particular door, verify eligibility and complete the assessments honestly. Otherwise, prioritize understanding and building things that can be explained and tested.

Use the [Evidence Tracker](EVIDENCE_TRACKER.csv) to record what has *actually* happened and the [Evidence Template](EVIDENCE_TEMPLATE.md) for original results. **No fake course completion dates, credentials, grades, conference attendances, proficiency levels or customer interviews.**
