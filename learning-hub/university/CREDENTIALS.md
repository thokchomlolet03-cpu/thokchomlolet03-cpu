# Academic credibility: what each learning record actually proves

[← University learning](README.md) · [Evidence tracker](EVIDENCE_TRACKER.csv) · [Template](EVIDENCE_TEMPLATE.md)

A university's brand can guide learning, but the **type of credential** matters just as much as the name.

## Evidence levels (different, not interchangeable)

| Record | Accurate claim | Avoid saying |
|---|---|---|
| Public lectures / OpenCourseWare | "Independently studied MIT OCW 6.004 and completed an original reproduction" | "Graduated from MIT" / "MIT-certified" |
| Harvard CS50 free course certificate | "Completed Harvard's CS50 [course] and earned a free CS50 Certificate" after actually earning it | "Harvard University degree" or "verified edX certificate" when not awarded |
| University of Helsinki MOOC | "Completed coursework in DevOps with Docker" or exact university credit wording **only if officially recorded** | "Earned Finnish university credit" without a university transcript/record |
| Open University OpenLearn badge | "Earned an OpenLearn digital badge/statement of participation" | "Open University accredited degree module" |
| Paid edX/Coursera verified certificate | State exact issuer, course and verification link, if actually obtained | "Completely free" or "University admitted student" |
| Your original GitHub lab | "Implemented/reproduced [specific test] and documented the result" | "Industrial production experience" if the test was synthetic |

### New credit and enrollment distinctions verified November 2026

- [Helsinki Python MOOC FAQ](https://programming-26.mooc.fi/faq/): everyone may study free; passing requires assessment, the completion certificate is available from the MOOC, and **recorded ECTS requires a subsequent Open University registration step**. The [2026 schedule](https://programming-26.mooc.fi/grading-and-exams/) and [Open University 2026–27 listings](https://www.helsinki.fi/en/admissions-and-education/open-university/open-university-studies-degree-programme/bachelors-programme-science) have **different end dates**; do not promise 2027 course material availability.
- [Helsinki Cyber Security Base 2026](https://cybersecuritybase.mooc.fi/descriptions/): a **3 ECTS MOOC** and a **separate 2 ECTS exam** are distinct offerings. Recorded credits require each course's rules and any examination arrangements to be completed.
- [Turku AI & Cybersecurity](https://www.utu.fi/fi/jatkuva-oppiminen/ai-cybersecurity-mooc): free standalone DigiCampus learning **earns neither an official certificate nor ECTS**. Only formally registered/assessed Open University studies are credit-bearing.
- [Turku registration instructions](https://www.utu.fi/en/open-university-studies/registration): no Finnish online banking credentials does not necessarily block starting the registration form; **eligibility for an official academic record** is still individual.
- [KAMK Cloud & Cybersecurity](https://opin.fi/en/study-options/KAMK/elements-of-cloud-and-cybersecurity/7913cfec-e909-4a48-9803-d323c8c5f6e1/implementations/792ec026-8453-4722-bdc7-a1d9b0b5833b): free course lists 2 credits, but automatic recording is described for **degree students**, not every foreign participant.
- [Metropolia Open UAS](https://www.metropolia.fi/en/study-at-metropolia/open-university): since 2 November 2026 enrollment normally requires a **Finnish personal identity code**; an advertised free 16-ECTS CCNA package is not automatically open to every person abroad.
- For an exact assessment of credit eligibility and course deadlines, use the [Finnish study opportunity audit](FINNISH_CREDIT_OPPORTUNITIES.md) instead of extrapolating from a headline ECTS value.

### Primary rules

- [Harvard CS50 Cybersecurity free-certificate terms](https://cs50.harvard.edu/cybersecurity/certificate/): 70%+ for each problem and final project.
- [Harvard CS50 AI free-certificate terms](https://cs50.harvard.edu/ai/certificate/): 70%+ on each project.
- [Harvard CS50 SQL free-certificate terms](https://cs50.harvard.edu/sql/certificate/): 70%+ for problems and final project.
- [University of Helsinki current DevOps with Docker](https://opin.fi/en/study-options/HY/devops-with-docker/32fc688c-76c7-42a7-b316-540a063deee7): free course participation, separate deadlines and registration requirements for ECTS-credit recording.
- [Open University Effective Communication](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/money-business/effective-communication-the-workplace): a non-academic-credit OpenLearn badge/statement after qualifying quizzes and coursework.
- [MIT OpenCourseWare](https://ocw.mit.edu/): material for independent study, without MIT credit or a free completion certificate.

## CV / LinkedIn wording examples — use only when earned

**A genuine completed free course certificate:** `CS50's Introduction to Cybersecurity — Harvard CS50 (free CS50 Certificate; earned YYYY-MM; verification URL)`.

**Open course self-study:** `Independent study — MIT OpenCourseWare 6.004 Computation Structures. Built [actual project], reviewed [actual source], evidence: [link]`.

**University-managed recorded credit:** `University of Helsinki, DevOps with Docker — [officially recorded ECTS credits if actually awarded], record identifier [private unless safe], YYYY-MM`.

**Unfinished:** `Currently studying [Course Name] independently, [official source]; lab and assessment work in progress`.

**Never make up dates, badges, earned credits or grades.** A course provider may restrict reproducing its graded assignment solutions publicly; link your **own independent variation** and respect published academic-integrity rules.

## Academic admission caveat

Short courses and public-course badges **do not automatically cure an undergraduate degree mismatch or establish graduate-program eligibility**. Different universities evaluate online qualifications, prerequisites, formal credits and mathematics backgrounds differently. Ask admissions to assess recognized degrees and official transcripts.

## Personal data

Evidence links are public. Do not upload exam account identifiers, verification email addresses, government ID, certificates containing private data, unrelated personal information, workplace screenshots, or school records to this public repository. Where public proof isn't appropriate, leave the private record outside GitHub and describe its type honestly.
