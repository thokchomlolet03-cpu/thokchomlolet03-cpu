# Conferences, communities, travel and participating in the world

[← Learning Hub](../README.md) · [Learning philosophy](../LEARNING_PHILOSOPHY.md) · [Professional communication](../personal-skills/PROFESSIONAL_COMMUNICATION.md)

**Goal:** meet engineers, researchers, designers and future collaborators; experience how people actually build things; practice discussing my work; and bring useful questions back to my learning projects.

This is a **discovery directory**, not a list of booked events or verified future dates. Costs, visas, leave, travel and organizer policies must be checked before commitment.

## Event selection without credential collecting

Consider:
1. **Fit:** does it address a real question in Linux, networks, AI, quantum, design, entrepreneurship, communication or languages?
2. **Participation:** can I ask a question, attend a workshop, meet a relevant practitioner or demonstrate a project?
3. **Learning quality:** are speakers and program verifiable; are talks educational rather than mostly sales pitches?
4. **Practicality:** travel permissions, visa rules, current residency, leave/shift schedule, entry fee, transport, accommodation and return-to-work risk.
5. **Cost and alternative:** if expensive, can I watch official recorded talks or join a local/online session with comparable learning value?
6. **Follow-through:** will I bring home one mechanism, one experiment, one contact or one new perspective?

A free online talk with good engagement may be more valuable than an expensive event attended solely for a badge or photo.

## Before / during / after an event

**Before:** read the speaker's work; prepare two specific questions and a short, accurate description of a personally verified project.

**During:** ask one thoughtful question when appropriate; listen for an explanation that challenges your current mental model. Introduce yourself respectfully without promoting an unbuilt company.

**After:** write a source-linked account of what you heard (fact vs inference), one project experiment and one appropriate follow-up to a person who agreed to connect.

## Skills beyond attendance

Public speaking, listening, respectful disagreement, networking follow-through, travel planning, cross-cultural communication and practicing other languages. These are all abilities to train; none requires abandoning introversion or incurring large financial risks.
