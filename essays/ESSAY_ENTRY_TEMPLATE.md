## For the public index

Copy a row into [README.md](README.md), ideally in reverse chronological order, then update [INDEX.csv](INDEX.csv) with a stable ID.

- Give readers the original publication link, not an editor URL or a draft URL.
- If you republish, make the relationship between original and cross-post clear.
- Separate storytelling from factual reporting. Do not imply a fictional narrator represents your biography.
- For science or technical opinions, distinguish hypotheses from experiments and link the **actual tests** instead of asserting imagined results.
- Check copyright and privacy before putting text, images, transcripts or unpublished personal details in a public repo.

Do not commit this filled template with private draft content unless it is intended to be public.
